## Oxide Etch Chamber Components

### Electrode Materials
| Material | Etch Rate in Oxide Process | Erosion Pattern | Lifetime | Notes |
|----------|---------------------------|-----------------|----------|--------|
| Silicon Carbide (SiC) | ~10-20 Å/min | Uniform | 1000+ hrs | Preferred for electrodes |
| Sapphire (Al₂O₃) | ~5-10 Å/min | Uniform | 2000+ hrs | Excellent selectivity |
| Aluminum (Al) | ~50-100 Å/min | Localized pits | 100-200 hrs | Not preferred |
