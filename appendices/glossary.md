# Glossary: Thermal Oxide Etch Terminology

## Terms & Definitions

### A
**ARDE**: Aspect Ratio Dependent Etching
- In silicon etch: narrow features etch slower (ion depletion)
- In oxide etch: wide features etch slower (radical depletion) — INVERSE ARDE

**Arrhenius Relationship**: Temperature dependence of etch rate
- ER(T) = ER₀ × exp(-Eₐ/kT)

### F
**Fluorocarbon Polymer**: C-F chain deposits on chamber walls and wafer surfaces
- Formed from CHF₃, CF₄ dissociation
- Removed via NF₃ plasma cleaning

**F-Atom**: Neutral fluorine radical
- Primary etchant for SiO₂
- Subject to depletion in high-rate regimes

**Flux**: Flow rate of particles (ions or radicals) to surface
- Measured in particles/(cm²·s)

### S
**Selectivity**: Ratio of etch rates between two materials
- Oxide-to-metal selectivity: >30:1 target
- Example: If oxide etches 600 Å/min and Al etches 20 Å/min, selectivity = 30:1

### T
**Temperature Sensitivity**: Rate of change of etch rate with temperature
- Oxide etch: ~7-10% per °C
- Metal etch (Book #16): ~4-6% per °C
- Silicon etch (Books 11-15): ~3-5% per °C
